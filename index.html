From one seed to a little ecosystem

Harvest seeds with the big button. Invest in garden gloves to earn more per tap, then unlock sprinklers and helper bots for automatic production. Prices rise with each purchase, so choose the next upgrade that suits your pace.

How to play

  1. Tap Harvest seeds to collect your first seeds.
  2. Buy an affordable upgrade in the shop.
  3. Balance stronger taps with automatic production.
  4. Reach garden milestones and keep growing.

Keep your progress

Progress is saved automatically in this browser. If you close the game, automatic upgrades can earn up to two hours of offline progress. Export a save before clearing your browser data or switching devices.

Your save belongs to this site and browser. Changing the site’s domain does not automatically move it; export and import your save to transfer it.
